7 · Quantum is one component. The loss rule and the verdict stay classical.

Plain analogy: a cloud sitting on all legal portfolios at once. Step 2 tilts the cloud toward cheap portfolios, step 3 stirs it so it can reach neighbouring portfolios. A classical optimiser adjusts how hard to tilt and stir. At the end we measure many times; the most frequent answer is the suggestion. QAOA is a heuristic; it does not guarantee the best answer.
