2 · The client

The German government’s public pension fund

Planned “gesetzliche Kapitalrente”: 2 % of wages invested by a public fund modelled on KENFO. The commission asks for broad diversification, costs up to 0.1 % a year and no guarantees.

Core

Broad index. Most of the money. Not our decision.

Satellite tilt

Three of six sectors, equal weight. This is what we decide.

3 · Source: Alterssicherungskommission, June 2026 (not yet law). Mandate numbers are our assumptions.

Client is the German government via the planned public fund. The 3-of-6 decision is a small satellite tilt; the core stays a broad index, as the commission demands. Profiles are chosen actively by each insured person, because age-based tilts would treat generations unequally. Default for people who do not choose: balanced (open assumption).
