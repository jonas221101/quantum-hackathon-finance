13 · Limits

No quantum advantage, and we say so

What we do show

  • A checked encoding that keeps the rules
  • Every result graded against the exact answer
  • A map of what flips the answer
  • Same numbers on a second laptop

What we do not show

  • That quantum beats classical: 20 options are trivial
  • Hardware results: simulator only, no noise
  • Real-fund fit: 60 months, US sectors, no liquidity, tax or ESG
  • Real mandate numbers: ours are assumptions

Equal weights and exactly 3 holdings are simplifications. The switching cost is a proxy, not a fee model.

14 · A small, transparent result beats a big unexplained one.

Say these before the jury does. The value is correct, rule-preserving encoding and verification, and a path to larger problems where enumeration is no longer possible. Even there, advantage is not established.
